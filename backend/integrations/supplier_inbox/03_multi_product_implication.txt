Harborview Foods is issuing a recall for a batch of Sesame Tahini (bulk) due to undeclared peanut allergen detected via supplier testing.

IMPORTANT: This ingredient was supplied to you for multiple finished products under your private label and may affect more than one SKU.

AFFECTED INGREDIENT
- Ingredient: Sesame Tahini (bulk)
- Ingredient Lot: HV-TAH-032526-B
- Received window: 03/27/2026–04/02/2026

POTENTIALLY AFFECTED FINISHED PRODUCTS (if produced using HV-TAH-032526-B)
- Private Label Hummus Classic, 10 oz (UPC 0 12345 67890 5)
- Private Label Hummus Roasted Garlic, 10 oz (UPC 0 12345 67891 2)
- Private Label Tahini Sauce, 8 oz (UPC 0 12345 67910 9)

ACTION REQUIRED
- Place affected finished products and any remaining tahini ingredient on hold
- Provide production dates / lot codes if available for your finished products

This is a Class I situation for customers with peanut allergy.
