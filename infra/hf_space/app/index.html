Pheromone
Recall Operating System
Built on AMD Instinct MI300X via vLLM + ROCm

When the FDA recalls food, no one is required to tell the people who bought it.

109 million units recalled in 2025. Pheromone fixes the gap.

What Pheromone does

  • Probabilistic provenance: no lot codes at checkout required.
  • Ingredient-level blast radius: contamination propagates through recipes.
  • Reassurance notifications: notify the safe with proof (prevents panic refunds).
  • Audit-ready compliance: deterministic logs + human approvals.
