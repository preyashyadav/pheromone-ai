AI Recall Operating System

Trace recalls. Notify the affected. Reassure the safe.

Pheromone is a multi-agent recall system that ingests recall notices, traces affected products through the supply chain, matches likely impacted transactions, and generates customer-safe communication workflows and audit-ready reporting.

What it does

  • Trace affected products through the supply chain graph.
  • Identify affected transactions with confidence tiers.
  • Notify impacted customers with customer-safe drafts.
  • Reassure safe customers using provenance evidence.
  • Generate audit-ready recall reports and a compliance event log.

The operational problem

Recalls happen, but purchase-level notification is often inconsistent. Customers don’t know whether they bought the recalled item, and unaffected customers may panic and discard safe food.

Unclear exposure

Customers often can’t map recall scope to what they actually purchased.

Unnecessary refunds

Broad alerts create waste, refunds, and support load for safe customers.

Operational coordination

Stores need a coordinated workflow from intake through pulls, comms, and reporting.

Audit pressure

Teams need a defensible record of trace evidence, approvals, and actions.

Workflow

A recall runbook from intake to communication, backed by trace evidence.

    Reassurance

    Most recall systems only notify affected customers. Pheromone also reassures customers who are not affected, using provenance evidence from the supply-chain graph.

    Affected
    Your purchase may be impacted.

    We traced your purchase to an exposure path (lot + shipment evidence). Next steps included.

    • Clear risk tier
    • Evidence-backed reasoning
    • Actionable next steps
    Safe
    Your purchase was traced to a clean batch.

    We can show non-exposure based on supply-chain provenance, reducing panic and refunds.

    • Provenance proof
    • Lower support volume
    • Trust-preserving messaging
